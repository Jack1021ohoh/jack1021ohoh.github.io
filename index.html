Applied Research Deep Learning · Sports Analytics

MLB Pitch Outcome Prediction & Sequence Optimization

Two-phase system for pitch-level modeling on MLB Statcast data: a multi-task transformer predicts pitch outcomes, then drives a Monte Carlo Tree Search sequencer that recommends optimal pitch selection.

  • Designed dual-encoder transformer fusing batter sequences and hierarchical pitcher appearances via cross-attention to predict outcomes, hit locations, and batted-ball physics
  • Surpassed LightGBM baseline on 2025 Statcast test data (top-4 recall 0.963 vs. 0.958, log-loss 1.048 vs. 1.114)
  • Engineered expectimax MCTS sequencer using the transformer as simulator with RE288 run-expectancy rewards to rank optimal pitch-zone actions

MLB Hit Prediction with Swing Data

Predictive modeling system classifying 5 MLB hit outcomes from raw pitch and swing biomechanics across 50,000+ swing records using a two-level stacking ensemble.

  • Built two-level stacking ensemble of CatBoost and DNN base learners with a logistic regression meta-model, achieving 0.74 AUC
  • Implemented automatic hyperparameter optimization with Optuna (TPE sampling)
  • Derived physics-informed features (Magnus effect, swing efficiency) and cross-validated importance via CatBoost and LightGBM
