About Me

I'm a Master's student in Data Science at Columbia University, graduating in December 2026. I like machine learning problems where the hard part is knowing whether the answer is right.

Building on my B.B.A. in Management Information Systems from National Sun Yat-sen University (GPA: 3.97/4.0), I've developed expertise in deep learning, machine learning, data mining, and big data analytics.

Most recently, as an AI/ML research intern at ASTA Health Tech, I built vital-sign forecasting with calibrated uncertainty bands, distilled a medical QA model into a smaller Qwen3-14B student, and read ECG waveforms from photos of patient monitors. Before that, I built LLM agents at TIFIN that ground financial explanations in live API data, and spent a year at Academia Sinica applying deep learning and federated learning to medical imaging and agriculture.

Beyond research, I'm passionate about sports analytics, especially baseball, where I enjoy uncovering insights from data to support decision-making and strategy.

MLB Pitch Outcome Prediction & Sequence Optimization

Two-phase system for pitch-level modeling on MLB Statcast data: a multi-task transformer predicts pitch outcomes, then drives a Monte Carlo Tree Search sequencer that recommends optimal pitch selection.

  • Designed dual-encoder transformer fusing batter sequences and hierarchical pitcher appearances via cross-attention to predict outcomes, hit locations, and batted-ball physics
  • Surpassed LightGBM baseline on 2025 Statcast test data (top-4 recall 0.963 vs. 0.958, log-loss 1.048 vs. 1.114)
  • Engineered expectimax MCTS sequencer using the transformer as simulator with RE288 run-expectancy rewards to rank optimal pitch-zone actions

Statistical Research Statistical Modeling · Sports Analytics

MLB Player Aging Curves with Survivorship Correction

Aging curves for five MLB performance metrics across 45 seasons of FanGraphs data, using GAMs with inverse probability weighting to correct for declining players dropping out of the sample before they retire.

  • Built GAM aging curves with a logistic survival model for IPW correction, beating the delta method by 8.7% (OPS) and 7.3% (wRC+) in MAE on held-out 2021–2025 seasons
  • Validated against simulated leagues with a known true curve, calibrated to 18 real-data moments: GAM beat the delta method on curve shape in 9–10 of 10 replicates (1.6–2.5× lower shape-based distance)
  • Found hitting and WAR peak at 27 and quantified the decline schedule (−0.65 WAR from 30 to 34); packaged as a Python library with 103 regression tests
